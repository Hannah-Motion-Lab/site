Brand and press kit

Everything on this page may be used to write about Vanth Labs and Hannah: logos, colors, type, the two names and how to write them, an official paragraph, and screenshots. Use the files unmodified. Questions: hello@vanthlabs.org.

Logo

The mark is a wing with a light at its root: Vanth, in Etruscan painting, is the winged figure who carries a torch and guides the way. In the wordmark the wing is attached to the V. Use the wordmark wherever there is room for the name, the mark alone where there is not (avatars, favicons, app icons).

  • Keep the wing attached. Do not separate the wing from the V, mirror it, or recolor the feathers individually.
  • One color at a time. Violet on dark, black on light, white on violet. No gradients, no outlines, no shadows other than the site's own glow.
  • Clear space. Leave at least the height of the V free around the wordmark.

Names, and how to write them

Vanth Labs
The company. Two words, capital V and L. Not VanthLabs, not Vanth Labs Inc., never just Labs. Pronounced "vanth", one syllable, rhymes with "tenth".
Hannah
The product. She, not it, when you mean the character; "Hannah" or "the Hannah app" when you mean the software. Made by Vanth Labs, not "Vanth Labs Hannah".
Together
"Hannah, by Vanth Labs" on first mention. After that, Hannah.

Official paragraph

Vanth Labs builds AI that has a body and stays on your machine. Its first product, Hannah, is an open source AI assistant with a voice, a 3D body and hands: she runs on your own computer, talks back in real time, gestures while she speaks with a text-to-motion model the company trained, and acts on your machine with your permission. Hannah installs with one command on Linux, macOS and Windows and is MIT-licensed. Vanth Labs was founded in 2026 in Lima, Peru.
