Hannah

A real-time AI companion that lives on your desktop.

She listens, answers out loud with lip-sync and emotion, gestures while she speaks and watches through your camera — running entirely on your own hardware.

$ curl -fsSL https://hannah-motion-lab.github.io/site/install.sh | bash

Linux x86_64 · ~103 MB self-contained AppImage

Capabilities

More than a chat window.

Every layer of the stack is local and streams in real time — from the first word you say to the gesture she answers with.

Speaks & listens

Local speech recognition (Whisper) and neural TTS (Kokoro). Talk over her mid-sentence — she yields instantly and listens.

Lip-sync & emotion

Each sentence drives visemes and one of eight emotional expressions on a VRM avatar rendered in real time.

in-house model

Co-speech gestures

A text-to-motion model trained by us turns every sentence into body language. She doesn't just talk — she moves.

Eyes on the room

A local vision model reads your camera feed and reacts only when something in the scene actually changes.

Hands on the machine

Opt-in terminal, app control and web search. Teach her new skills with plain markdown files — destructive commands ask first.

Private by default

Everything runs on your GPU. Audio never touches disk, transcripts never touch logs, the backend never leaves localhost.

Requirements

Light enough to live with you.

Operating system
Linux x86_64 today. Windows and macOS builds compile through CI on every release tag.
GPU
NVIDIA with CUDA recommended. AMD or CPU-only also works — voice gets slower.
Brain
Ollama with qwen2.5:7b, plus nomic-embed-text for long-term memory.
Floating overlay
Works on Hyprland, KDE, GNOME, XFCE and friends via XWayland. wmctrl/kdotool unlock moving her by voice.

≤ 16 GB

of VRAM fits the entire stack: LLM, embeddings, TTS, ASR, vision and motion.

~5 GB the qwen2.5:7b brain
~103 MB the app itself
